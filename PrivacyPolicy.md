# Privacy Policy — Letterboxd Alternative Sources

**Last updated:** 19 August 2026

## 1. Overview

This browser extension ("Extension") adds links to alternative search and streaming sources on Letterboxd film pages. It works entirely in your browser and does not collect, store, or sell your personal data.

This Extension is an independent project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Letterboxd.

## 2. Data the Extension accesses

The Extension only accesses:

- The film page you have open on `letterboxd.com` — specifically the film's title and release year, read from the page

This information is used solely to:

- Build the search URLs for the sources you have configured
- Display those links on the film page

The film title and year are used at the moment the page is displayed and are not retained afterwards.

## 3. Data collection, storage, and sharing

The Extension:

- Does not collect personal data (such as your name, email, account info, or browsing history).
- Does not send any data to external servers controlled by the developer or third parties.
- Does not use analytics or tracking tools.
- Does not share any data with advertisers or other third parties.

All logic runs locally in your browser.

## 4. Cookies and local storage

The Extension:

- Does not set its own cookies.
- Does not store data in sync storage, and does not store data for tracking or profiling.

The Extension does use your browser's local extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`) for one purpose: to save the list of sources you configure on the options page (each source's display name, base search URL, icon URL, additional text, and query formatting options). This list stays on your device, is never transmitted anywhere, and is removed when you uninstall the Extension. You can view, edit, export, or delete it at any time from the options page.

Clicking a source link opens that third-party site in a new tab. Once you are on that site, its own privacy policy and cookies apply — the Extension has no involvement beyond opening the link.

## 5. Changes to this policy

If the Extension's functionality changes in a way that affects privacy, this policy will be updated and a new version will be published along with the updated extension.
